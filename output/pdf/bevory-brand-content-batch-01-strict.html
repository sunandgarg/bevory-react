Description

Vallonne Vineyards is an Indian boutique wine estate founded by Shailendra Pai in 2009 at Kavnai, Igatpuri, near Nashik. Its official portfolio lists Chenin Blanc, Riesling, Viognier, Cabernet Sauvignon, Merlot, and Syrah, plus a Cabernet Sauvignon rosé and a dessert wine made with sundried grapes.

The range runs from aromatic white grapes such as Riesling and Viognier to estate-grown red grapes including Cabernet Sauvignon, Merlot, and Syrah. The dessert wine's sundried-grape method gives it a separate, sweeter style from the still wines.

How to Enjoy & Pair

Serve white and rosé wines chilled, and give the reds a little air in a wide wine glass. Try them with malai chicken tikka, tandoori crab, Thai curries, or mushroom dishes; check BevOry for city-level prices and availability.

Charosa

Wine
Logo review: Charosa's official vineyard domain is identified; the live page did not expose a stable logo asset URL during this review.
official domain found; logo asset pending verification

Description

Charosa Vineyards is an Indian wine estate in Charosa village, in Nashik's Dindori sub-region, spread across about 230 acres. Its published portfolio has used three tiers—Pleasures, Selections, and Reserve—and its production material identifies open-tank fermentation; the estate's documented grapes include Tempranillo, Cabernet Sauvignon, Shiraz, Sauvignon Blanc, and Viognier.

Tasting Notes & Style

The range is bottle-specific: Reserve Tempranillo is described with ripe red-fruit, vanilla, coconut, and chocolate notes; Reserve Cabernet Sauvignon with ripe fruit, black olive, vanilla, and balanced tannins; and Selection Viognier with floral and apricot notes. Check the exact vintage for the final aroma and body description.

How to Enjoy & Pair

Serve Charosa whites chilled and let Reserve reds breathe in a wide wine glass. Try the whites with paneer tikka or grilled fish, and the Reserve Tempranillo with lamb rogan josh; check BevOry for city-level prices and availability.
