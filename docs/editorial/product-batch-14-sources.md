# Product editorial batch 14 — source ledger

Thirty existing catalogue slugs, with no overlap against batches 01–13. Producer material is the main source for identity, production and sensory notes. The archived Glenfiddich Select Cask and Johnnie Walker Swing rely in part on specialist/retailer records where current producer product pages are unavailable; their copy avoids unsupported batch-specific claims. Pairings and serve suggestions are BevOry editorial judgments, not producer endorsements. Where a bottle's edition, strength or origin can differ by market, its physical label remains the authority.
