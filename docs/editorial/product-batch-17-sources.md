# Product editorial batch 17 — source ledger

Thirty existing, previously uncovered product slugs. Producer pages support identity, process and a house sensory profile; the Indian food matches and concise tasting language are editorial synthesis, not a claim that every vintage or batch was sampled. Bottling, blend and cask details can change. The catalogue names “Bottega Rose Gold Prosecco” and “Luxardo Bitter Bianco Wine” are imprecise: Rose Gold is Pinot Nero sparkling rosé, and Bitter Bianco is a herbal aperitivo, not wine. The public name remains recognisable while category and copy correct the distinction. Long names need shortened meta titles to fit the requested 60-character ceiling.
