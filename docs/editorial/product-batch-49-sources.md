# Product editorial batch 49 — source ledger

Thirty existing catalogue slugs were selected because they had no prior researched profile. Producer evidence was checked on 24 September 2026. Tasting language is editorial interpretation of the producer's stated product style and ingredients, not a claim that BevOry lab-tested these bottles. Pairings and serving suggestions are editorial recommendations.

| Catalogue group | Primary evidence | Boundary used in public copy |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Povero (7) | [Cantine Povero catalogue](https://cantinepovero.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/Cantine_Povero_Catalogo-IT.pdf), [Langhe range](https://cantinepovero.com/product-category/langhe/), [Moscato](https://cantinepovero.com/en/product/campo-del-palio-moscato-dasti-docg-2/), [Dolcetto](https://cantinepovero.com/en/product-category/vini-rossi-en/dolcetto-en/) | Broad catalogue labels do not establish a specific vintage, sub-label, or barrel programme. “Barberesco Batu” maps to producer's Batù Barbaresco spelling. |
| Geist (5) | [Geist beer range](https://www.geist.in/beers), [Kamacitra](https://www.geist.in/beer-catalogue/geist-kamacitra), [Repeat Strong](https://www.geist.in/beer-catalogue/geist-repeat-strong) | Style, yeast, fruit and hop claims follow the named producer beers; no invented hop cultivar or barrel ageing. |
| Woodpecker (5) | [SOM Woodpecker range](https://somindia.com/product/woodpecker-crest/), [SOM product portfolio](https://somindia.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/investpresq1fy26.pdf) | Glide/Crest and can/bottle are not described as five separate recipes. Producer page has generic process headings; its “distillation” heading was not treated as a beer process. |
| Bottega (4) | [Limoncino](https://www.bottegaspa.com/collezioni/limoncino/), [Pinot Grigio Venezia DOC](https://www.bottegaspa.com/en/collections/pinot-grigio-venezia-doc/), [Cappuccino](https://www.bottegaspa.com/en/collections/cappuccino/), [Valpolicella range](https://www.bottegaspa.com/le-collezioni/vini/valpolicella/) | Cream and standard lemon liqueur are kept distinct; Valpolicella Classico is not called Amarone. |
| Pursue (5) | [Pursue's five flavours](https://pursuedrinks.com/pages/flavours), [Pursue home](https://pursuedrinks.com/) | Catalogue typos “Mango &Chill” and “Stawberry” are corrected to producer flavour names. No unsupported ABV, sugar, calorie or base-spirit claims. |
| DesmondJi (4) | [Producer offerings](https://desmondji.com/) | Mahua, Agave 51, Orange Liqueur and Margarita are distinct. No Indian agave spirit is called tequila; Agave 51 is not the oak-finished Gold release. |

All 30 source slugs were compared against earlier batch exports by the batch test. Metadata lengths, non-empty schema fields, banned copy and registration are tested as well.
