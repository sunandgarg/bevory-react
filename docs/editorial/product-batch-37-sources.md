## Identity and claim cautions

- The Havana Club Añejo Blanco listing was excluded because the Cuban and Puerto Rican brands share a name; its origin could not be established safely from the slug alone.
- The three limited Johnnie Walker entries are discontinued or market-specific. Producer records confirm their identities, but detailed sensory lines are editorial profiles, not current official tasting-sheet quotations. A bottle-level check is recommended.
- Grant's Distinction and Family Reserve have weaker public liquid specifications than Triple Wood. No specific cask or age claim has been transferred from Triple Wood to those expressions.
- Royal Stag Barrel Select's exact cask regime is not asserted; its name alone does not prove a particular wood or age.
- Code publication does not imply the production application or database has been redeployed. Search indexing and ranking are not guaranteed.
