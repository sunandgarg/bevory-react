## Identity and fact follow-ups

- Bodega Juan: the catalogue product says "Bodega Juan Gill Pedrera Rosado". The guide covers producer Bodegas Juan Gil only; the product's precise Pedrera identity needs manual confirmation. Tasting notes from the separate Juan Gil Rosado were not transferred to it.
- Raya: an unrelated "White Wine Platinum" product shares the imported brand association. This guide covers Raya hard seltzer only; correct the product association separately.
- O'Be: some trade material suggests operations stopped. The guide describes the brand's historical range and makes no current-availability claim.
- Schweppes: the catalogue lists a Mojito ready-to-drink expression. Its producer and trademark relationship in this market need confirmation; do not equate it with ordinary tonic water.
- Rawsons: catalogue spelling lacks the apostrophe and "Retreat". The guide uses the established Rawson's Retreat identity, but the individual product identity should be checked.
- The 26 unresolved logos and all image usage rights need review before publishing. Existing stored images were not overwritten.
