# Product batch 52: source and scope notes

Thirty previously uncovered catalogue slugs: 14 Magic Moments, 13 McDowell's and 3 Royal Challenge. The slugs remain unchanged; display names normalize spelling and identify a range rather than a single flavour where the catalogue is broad. Sensory language is editorial guidance informed by the named flavour/style, not a laboratory assay or guarantee that every historical pack tastes identical. Pack labels govern ABV, ingredients and exact expression.

## Producer evidence

- [Radico Khaitan: Magic Moments](https://radicokhaitan.com/products/magic-moments/) — 2006 launch, grain base and triple distillation.
- [Radico Khaitan: Remix](https://radicokhaitan.com/products/magic-moments-remix/) — eight named Remix flavours, including lemongrass and ginger and grapefruit and watermelon.
- [Radico Khaitan: Verve](https://radicokhaitan.com/products/magic-moment-verve-3/) — five-stage filtration and Verve flavour line.
- [Radico Khaitan: Dazzle Vanilla](https://radicokhaitan.com/products/dazzle-silver/) and [2023 annual report](https://radicokhaitan.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/09/Annual-Report-RKL-2023.pdf) — Madagascan vanilla; Dazzle Gold and Vanilla are different expressions.
- [Radico Khaitan: ready-to-drink vodka cocktails](https://radicokhaitan.com/products/electra-rtd-2/) — Mojito and Cosmopolitan among cocktail flavours. Cola names are supported by catalogue labels only; no exact recipe or ABV is asserted.
- [Diageo India: McDowell's & Co Distiller's Batch](https://www.diageoindia.com/~/media/Files/D/Diageo-V2/Diageo-India/news-and-media/press-release/2024/risen-from-the-mist-mcdowells-co-reinvents-its-legacy.pdf) — Nashik, sequential ex-bourbon/virgin oak and Cabernet Sauvignon/Shiraz casks, 6,000-bottle release and producer tasting notes.
- [Diageo India: X Series](https://www.diageoindia.com/en/news-and-media/press-releases/2024/x-series-from-mcdowells-co-a-premium-new-range-of-vodka-rum-and-gin-hits-the-market) — single-grain vodka process; citrus dry gin; French lemon/lime with Indian white rum; Jamaican and Indian dark-rum blend. Broad McDowell's catalogue labels are not automatically equated with X Series packs.
- [Diageo India: Platinum launch](https://www.diageoindia.com/en/news-and-media/press-releases/archive/results-q-1-fy11) — Platinum described as 100% grain whisky.
- [Diageo India: Royal Challenge](https://www.diageoindia.com/en/brands/brand-explorer/royal-challenge-whisky) and [2024–25 annual report](https://www.diageoindia.com/~/media/Files/D/Diageo-V2/Diageo-India/investors/financials/results-reports-and-presentations/united-spirits-limited-annual-report-2024-25.pdf) — Scotch/Indian malt/grain blend and charred American oak; American Pride's bourbon-and-Indian-spirit construction.

## Editorial cautions

- The generic `Magic Moments Remix` and `Dazzle Vodka` listings are range-level entries. They do not identify a single flavour.
- The generic McDowell's entries do not inherit the newer X Series recipe unless the pack explicitly says X Series.
- The `McDowell's Single Malt Whisky` listing is matched to the producer's documented Distiller's Batch, but should be checked against the live bottle/pack before adding a vintage or batch-specific claim.
- Ready-to-drink cocktail entries should never be described as equivalent in strength to straight vodka.
- Repeated brand products in the catalogue may be pack or naming variants. The batch does not merge records or alter prices.
