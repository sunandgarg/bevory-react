# Product batch 42: producer evidence

Prepared 24 September 2026. These 30 records map to existing catalogue slugs. Copy is original editorial paraphrase, not copied producer text. Sensory and pairing guidance must be checked against the actual imported bottle where a catalogue title omits expression or vintage. No price or stock claim is made.

Publication caveat: catalogue names for Captain Morgan Rum, Blue Nun Pink, Kendall-Jackson Red Wine Blend and some B&G entries do not uniquely identify a liquid. These need exact bottle-label matching before highly specific sensory or production claims can be considered verified. Long catalogue names use a shorter title to stay within 60 characters.
